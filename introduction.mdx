Most subscription businesses only learn a customer is leaving when the cancellation email lands in their inbox — by then it's too late. Churnlane changes that. It monitors every customer hourly, compares their behaviour against their own baseline, and flags anyone whose engagement is slipping. When it finds a risk, it tells you exactly why in plain English and drafts a personalised win-back action ready for your approval. You stay in control; Churnlane does the work.

## How Churnlane Works

Churnlane runs a continuous four-step loop for every customer in your account.

1. **It spots them.** Every account is re-scored hourly against its own historical patterns, not industry averages. A customer who normally logs in daily but hasn't in a week stands out immediately.
2. **It tells you why.** You see one plain-English sentence — for example, "Onboarding stalled at SSO setup three weeks ago" — with ranked evidence underneath so you can judge for yourself.
3. **It drafts the fix.** Churnlane writes a win-back email, a targeted offer, or a suggested call agenda tailored to that specific customer's situation.
4. **You approve, it sends.** Nothing goes to your customer until you review and approve it. Actions go out through your own Stripe account, inbox, or helpdesk so they feel native to your brand.

## Integrations

Churnlane pulls data from the tools you already use.

**Billing providers** — Connect [Stripe](/integrations/stripe), [Paddle](/integrations/paddle), or [Chargebee](/integrations/chargebee) to bring in subscription events, payment failures, and plan changes. Stripe connects in one click via OAuth; Paddle and Chargebee use a webhook and API key.

**Product and support tools** — Connect [PostHog](/integrations/posthog) or the Churnlane SDK to track in-app events, [Intercom](/integrations/intercom) to read support sentiment, and [HubSpot](/integrations/hubspot) to keep your CRM in sync with every risk flag and win-back action.
