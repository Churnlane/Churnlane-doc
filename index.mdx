## Get Up and Running

Follow these four steps to go from zero to your first flagged account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Billing Provider">
    Link Stripe, Paddle, or Chargebee so Churnlane can detect billing risk signals — failed payments, plan downgrades, and upcoming renewals — from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the SDK">
    Add the Churnlane SDK to your app with `npm install @churnlane/sdk` and start sending product usage events. A script tag embed is also available for no-build-step setups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review At-Risk Accounts">
    Open the dashboard at app.churnlane.com/overview to see every flagged customer, their risk score, and the plain-English explanation of why they're slipping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve Win-Back Actions">
    Churnlane drafts a tailored email, offer, or call prompt for each at-risk account. Review it, edit if you like, and approve — nothing reaches your customer until you say so.
  </Step>
</Steps>
