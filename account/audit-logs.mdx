Every action Churnlane takes — whether it runs autonomously, after a team member clicks Approve, or is escalated because it exceeded a permission limit — is written to the audit log. The log gives you a complete, tamper-evident record of what happened in your workspace, when it happened, who was involved, and what the outcome was. Use it for compliance reviews, internal post-mortems, or tuning your approval rules over time.

<Note>
  Audit logs are a **Scale plan** feature. On Starter and Growth, high-level outcome summaries are available in your email digests, but the full structured audit log with export capability requires Scale. See [Churnlane Plans](/account/plans) for details.
</Note>

## What Is Logged

Every log entry captures the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Timestamp** | Date and time the action was executed or escalated, in UTC |
| **Action type** | One of: Retention Email, Discount, Credit, Plan Change, Free Month |
| **Customer account** | The customer name, ID, and plan at the time of the action |
| **Triggered by** | `Autonomous` if the action ran without human approval; the approving team member's name and email if manually approved |
| **Outcome** | `Sent`, `Skipped`, `Failed`, or `Escalated` (exceeded permission limit) |
| **Amount** | For discounts: the percentage applied. For credits: the value applied. Blank for other action types. |
| **Churn score at time of action** | The score that triggered the intervention |
| **Notes** | For escalated actions, the permission limit that was exceeded and the proposed value |

Actions that were placed in the approval queue and then **dismissed** (not approved) are also logged, with an outcome of `Dismissed` and the team member who dismissed them.

## Viewing Logs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Audit Logs">
    Go to **Settings → Security → Audit Logs** in your Churnlane dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Date Range">
    Use the date picker to select the period you want to review. The log displays the most recent 90 days by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by Action Type or Account">
    Use the filter bar to narrow entries by action type (e.g., show only Discount entries), by triggered-by (Autonomous vs. a specific team member), or by customer account name or ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Entries">
    Click any log entry to expand it and see the full detail view, including the churn signals that were active at the time and a link to the customer's account in Churnlane.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exporting Logs

You can export any filtered view of the audit log as a CSV file for offline analysis, compliance reporting, or sharing with stakeholders who don't have Churnlane access.

To export:

1. Apply any filters you want (date range, action type, etc.)
2. Click **Export CSV** in the top-right of the log view
3. The CSV will download to your browser immediately if the result set is under 10,000 rows. For larger exports, Churnlane will email a download link to the exporting user within a few minutes.

The CSV preserves all fields shown in the table view, including the full timestamp in ISO 8601 format.

## Log Retention

Audit log entries are retained for **12 months** from the date of the action. Entries older than 12 months are automatically purged.

If you need logs retained for longer — for example, to meet a compliance framework's requirements — export the relevant period as CSV before the 12-month mark and store it in your own systems.

<Tip>
  Build a monthly habit of reviewing your audit log alongside your churn metrics. Patterns in the log — for example, a high rate of `Escalated` discount actions — often signal that your permission limits or approval rules need tuning. The log tells you what happened; your churn numbers tell you whether it worked.
</Tip>
