Churnlane offers three plans built for different stages of SaaS growth. Every plan includes the core churn scoring engine and cancel flow agent — the difference is in how far you can automate, how many customers you're tracking, and what enterprise controls you need. This page explains what's in each plan and how to decide which one fits your team today.

<Note>
  Churnlane is currently in **private beta**. Pricing is not yet publicly available. Join the waitlist at [churnlane.com](https://churnlane.com) to get early access and be notified when pricing opens.
</Note>

## Choosing a Plan

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Starter" icon="seedling">
    **Best for:** Early-stage SaaS teams establishing their churn baseline.

    You get the full scoring engine and cancel flow agent, plus email digests and approval guardrails. Every at-risk account and every proposed action goes through your approval queue — no automation fires without your say-so. Use Starter to learn how Churnlane reads your data before you add any automation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Growth" icon="chart-line">
    **Best for:** Teams running real retention playbooks with recurring churn pressure.

    Growth adds autonomous interventions, payment recovery, and the live AI support agent. Once you trust the signals, you can let Churnlane execute retention emails, discounts, and credits without manual review on every item. The approval queue is still there when you need it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Scale" icon="building">
    **Best for:** Companies with significant recurring revenue and compliance or security requirements.

    Scale adds custom action permissions (fine-grained caps on what automation can do), SSO for your team, full audit logs, a dedicated environment, and access to Churnlane's solutions engineering team for onboarding and ongoing support.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Upgrading

You can upgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing** in the Churnlane dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately — new features become available as soon as the billing change processes.

If you're considering Scale and want to discuss your setup, tracked customer volume, or enterprise requirements before committing, contact us at **support@churnlane.com** and we'll connect you with the right person.

  During private beta, plan changes may require a brief conversation with the team. Reach out via your waitlist confirmation email or at support@churnlane.com and we'll sort it quickly.
